Local-first by design

Your server credentials stay on your device.

Passwords and key-encryption secrets are stored in Apple Keychain; imported private keys are encrypted on device. Connections go directly from your device to your server.

Optional Connection Map geolocation is off by default. If enabled, public source IP addresses are sent to ipwho.is for approximate locations. agent-ssh has no advertising SDK and anonymous diagnostics are opt-in. Read the privacy policy.
